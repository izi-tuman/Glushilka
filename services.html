Services

From the first sketch to long-term care.

We offer a focused set of services, each led by a senior engineer. We can take a project from idea to launch, modernise something that has outgrown its original design, or simply keep an important system running smoothly.

Web platform development

Most of what we build is a web application of some kind: a customer portal, an internal operations tool, a booking system, a dashboard that ties together half a dozen other systems. We build these as well-structured, thoroughly tested applications that your team can understand and extend, rather than fragile one-offs.

We are careful about the foundations — clear data models, sensible boundaries between components, and an interface that is genuinely usable on the devices your people actually use. The result is software that feels solid on day one and stays easy to change on day five hundred.

  • Customer-facing portals and self-service tools
  • Internal operations and admin applications
  • Accessible, responsive interfaces tested on real devices
  • Automated test suites and continuous delivery from the start

Cloud integration & infrastructure

Running software reliably is its own discipline. We design cloud architectures that match the size of the problem — not the size of the marketing slide — and we describe every environment as code so it can be reviewed, reproduced, and recovered. Deployments become routine, predictable events instead of nervous occasions.

We are equally comfortable setting up a clean new environment or untangling one that has grown organically over the years. Our goal is always the same: infrastructure that is understandable, observable, and affordable to run.

  • Cloud architecture sized to your actual load and budget
  • Infrastructure as code for reproducible environments
  • CI/CD pipelines with safe, automated deployments
  • Monitoring, alerting, and sensible backup and recovery

Data pipelines & reporting

Decisions are only as good as the numbers behind them. We build the pipelines that collect, clean, and combine data from your various systems, and the warehouses and reports that turn it into something people can trust. When a figure looks wrong, we make it possible to trace exactly where it came from.

We favour transparent, testable transformations over opaque magic. Every important number should be explainable, and every pipeline should fail loudly rather than quietly producing nonsense.

  • ETL and data integration across multiple sources
  • Data warehousing and well-modelled reporting layers
  • Dashboards and scheduled reports your team will actually use
  • Data quality checks and clear lineage

Legacy modernisation

Plenty of valuable software is quietly holding a business together while becoming harder and riskier to change. We specialise in modernising these systems without the drama of a big-bang rewrite. We learn what the existing system really does, add a safety net of tests, and improve it in careful, reversible steps.

Sometimes the right answer is to replace a component; sometimes it is to leave a working part well alone. We are honest about which is which, because a rewrite that isn't needed is just risk with a nicer logo.

  • Assessment of an existing system's health and risks
  • Incremental refactoring with a growing test safety net
  • Careful migration of data and integrations
  • Knowledge transfer so your team is never left in the dark

Support & maintenance

Software needs looking after. Our support retainers cover the unglamorous but essential work of keeping a system healthy: applying updates, watching for problems before they become outages, and making the steady stream of small improvements that keep software pleasant to use.

We agree clear response times up front and report honestly on what we have done. Many clients start with a single project and stay with us for years on a support arrangement, simply because it removes a worry from their week.

  • Proactive monitoring and maintenance
  • Security patching and dependency updates
  • Agreed response times and transparent reporting
  • A steady cadence of small improvements
Engagement models

Ways to work with us

Fixed-scope project

A clearly defined piece of work with a written plan, a milestone schedule, and an agreed price. Best when the goal is well understood from the start.

Ongoing team

A small Arcwell team working alongside yours on a continuing roadmap, with priorities reviewed together each iteration. Best for evolving products.

Support retainer

A set amount of capacity each month for maintenance, improvements, and the occasional urgent fix. Best for systems that need a safe pair of hands.
